Separate pieces of solid up to the size limit become props (trees, stalagmites, boulders, broken pillars, chosen by the zone's law). They don't lower the ceiling. Caves get domes; halls and rooms get one height per space.

Connectivity

The world is 40×40 tiles: a 2×2 arrangement of 5×5-cell blocks (4×4 tiles per cell). Each block is split into rectangles with one of Boris The Brave's irreducible 5×5 subdivisions (53 shapes × 8 orientations). Each rectangle is a zone with a law. Border pips are floor if either zone wants floor; numbers blend across the band.

POV

W/S or ↑/↓ move, A/D or ←/→ turn, Q/E strafe. Click the view if keys don't respond. Walls take their zone's colour.

Zone graph

Nodes are zones, numbered by steps from your zone (red ring). Links: grey = joined by generation, orange = tunnel, blue dashed = loop. White nodes have no floor; solid wall zones are hidden.
