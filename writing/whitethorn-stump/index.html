whitethorn stump

2026

theres a megalithically large stump cut down cleanly in the centre of the town, dying outwards from the tree are ancient roots and plants. withered from neglect and lack of sun. buildings are built up over the dying vista. earth has been disturbed, and stones overturned. at the outskirts there are massive stones pointing towards the sky above, filled with white fluffy clouds on a background of pure bright sky blue. the boulders present are so large and intimidating, you can tell it would take machines or gods to get them this high up.

the town is over run with carion feeder birds. they're picking at old bones, tonguing the last crumb of marrow from the cracked white porcelien. once theyre done feeding, they fly off into the clouds. they all seem happy to see you.

most of the buildings are non descript, too decayed to be recognisable.

glow in the dark cats haunt the grounds. friendly but feral. they let you touch their fur but stroke too long and you might get bit. they're temperamental and not used to people. the streets light up at dusk when the colony of kitties climbs out of their daytime hiding places and roams the streets to hunt. its like watching a lavalamp in fast motion.
